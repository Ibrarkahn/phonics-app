Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-02-25

Phonics Fun is designed for children’s phonics practice. We do not ask for names, accounts, or contact details inside the app.

What data we collect

  • Anonymous analytics events (if enabled): basic usage signals such as week opened, week completed, and sounds played.
  • Local device storage: progress (stars/completed weeks) is saved on your device using localStorage so the app can remember what you’ve completed.

How we use this data

  • To understand which weeks and sounds are used most often.
  • To improve the learning experience and fix issues.

Data sharing

We do not sell your data. Analytics data (if enabled) is processed by Firebase Analytics.

Your choices

  • You can reset your progress from the in-app Settings page.
  • If you do not want analytics, you can remove the Firebase analytics integration from the app build.
